Frequently Asked Questions

What is Adobe Edge Delivery Services?
Edge Delivery Services is Adobe's platform for building fast, easy-to-author websites that serve content from the aem.live backend.
Does this FAQ generate structured data?
Yes. When the accordion uses the FAQ variant, the block JavaScript injects a schema.org FAQPage JSON-LD script into the page head.
Will Google show it as a rich result?
The FAQPage markup makes the questions eligible for FAQ rich results. Validate the page with the Google Rich Results Test.
