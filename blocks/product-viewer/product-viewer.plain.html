Features and advantages

  • Turbo Ring and Turbo Chain (250 switches, recovery time < 20 milliseconds)And support network backup through RSTP/STP protocols.
  • IEEE 802.3bt PoE supports up to 90W output per port.
  • Wide range of power input options suitable for flexible deployment
  • Its compact and flexible housing design allows for easy installation in confined spaces.
  • Supports MXstudio, enabling easy visualization of industrial network management.
  • Certified to IEC 62443-4-2 Safety Level 2
