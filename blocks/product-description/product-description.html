Introduction

The EDS-4008 series includes several 8-port managed high-speed Ethernet switches, with options for four 1 Gbps uplink ports or two 100M ST/SC fiber optic ports. This series also offers models with optional four 10/100BaseT(X) 802.3af (PoE), 802.3at (PoE+), and 802.3bt compatible Ethernet PoE ports for connecting high-bandwidth PoE devices.

Backup Ethernet technologies (such as Turbo Ring, Turbo Chain, and RSTP/STP) can increase system reliability and improve network backbone availability. The EDS-4008 series is specifically designed for demanding applications (such as video and process monitoring, ITS, and DCS systems), and all applications can benefit from a scalable backbone.

 

Certified to IEC 62443-4-2 Safety Level 2

The EDS-(G)4000 series models are IEC 62443-4-2 Security Level 2 certified (https://certificates.iecee.org/#/deliverables/CERT/1605016/view), which helps our customers meet the compliance requirements for secure industrial network design.
